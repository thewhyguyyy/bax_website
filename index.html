Who is BAX

BAX is creating India's first professional ecosystem for differently-abled athletes. From leagues and training to media and technology — everything is designed to create real opportunity.

The League

Professional tournament structures designed for visibility and career progression.

Media Hub

Documentaries, series and athlete stories that put differently-abled talent in the spotlight.

Tech Platform

A unified digital identity and athlete passport powering recruitment and performance history.

Community

A nationwide network connecting athletes, coaches, families and fans.

Our Mission

To discover, develop, and elevate differently-abled athletes across India.

Discover Talent

Identifying hidden potential in every corner of India through our nationwide digital selection process.

Build Champions

Providing elite coaching and sports-science support to bridge the gap between amateur and professional.

Create Opportunity

Ensuring high-level sports exposure is accessible to everyone, regardless of background or geography.
